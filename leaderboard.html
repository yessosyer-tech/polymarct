THE CONVICTION INDEX

Consensus is cheap.
Conviction is expensive.

Traders are ranked on risk adjusted performance across resolved markets, not on how much money they moved. Size alone is not skill, and a leaderboard that rewards size is a leaderboard that rewards wash trading.

WALLETS ARE DISPLAYED TRUNCATED. NO POSITION SIZES, BALANCES OR IDENTITIES ARE EXPOSED.

METHOD

How the index resists gaming.

WHAT COUNTS
RESOLVED ONLYopen positions score nothing
RISK ADJUSTEDreturn per unit of variance
ENTRY TIMINGearly correct beats late correct
BREADTHacross categories, not one market
CONSISTENCYsustained over a rolling window
WHAT DOES NOT COUNT
RAW VOLUMEexcluded as a ranking input
SELF MATCHED FLOWdetected and discarded
NEAR CERTAIN MARKETSweighted down by price
SYBIL CLUSTERSfunding graph clustering
LATE PILE ONscored against entry price

Anti gaming is a product requirement, not a nice to have. A leaderboard is an incentive, and every incentive gets farmed.
