BRAND SYSTEM

The mark is
an argument.

Two sides of the order book, facing each other. The taller bracket is the side holding the weight of the money, the shorter one is the other outcome, and the acid bar standing in the gap between them is the last price. When the market reprices, the logo reprices.

01 / THE MARK

The book.

Pure geometry, no type inside the mark, no effects. Acid is reserved for the price line, never the mass: it is the only saturated colour in the identity and it marks the one thing the product sells.
