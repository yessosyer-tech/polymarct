THE VERDICT ENGINE

Reality decides.
The engine records it.

Resolution is the product. Everything upstream is just pricing. The Verdict Engine takes an event, reads a pre committed source, verifies it, resolves the market and settles the positions. The question cannot change after trading opens.

01

EVENT OCCURS

The real world does something. The market does not need to be told; the window was fixed at creation.

02

DATA SOURCE

The source named in the market is read at the resolution time. One primary, one declared fallback, nothing improvised.

03

VERIFICATION

The reading is checked against the criteria, cross read against the fallback, and published with the raw value attached.

04

RESOLUTION

The verdict is written on chain with its evidence. A dispute window opens before anything settles.

05

SETTLEMENT

Winning positions settle in USDC on Arc. Deterministic finality means settled is settled.

CONTESTED OUTCOMES

When reality is argued about.

DISPUTE WINDOW

24 hours between verdict and settlement. Any holder of a position in the market can post a dispute bond and cite the specific criterion they believe was misread.

REVIEW

A disputed market goes to review against the original wording only. Not against what people think should have happened, not against a better question someone wishes had been asked.

FINAL VERDICT

The final verdict is published with the evidence and the reasoning. A successful dispute returns the bond and corrects the resolution. A failed dispute forfeits it.

UNRESOLVABLE

If the source is discontinued and the fallback cannot answer, the market voids and positions are returned at cost. A wrong verdict is worse than no verdict.

THE ORACLE

The oracle has spoken.

Used sparingly, and never as a mascot. When a market resolves the verdict is stated plainly, with the reading that produced it. When a market is genuinely undecided the honest output is 51 / 49, and the honest thing to say is that the oracle is confused.

RISK AND ELIGIBILITY

The design is aggressive.
The claims are not.

RISK

Every position is collateralised and settled in USDC on Arc, and no other asset or chain is accepted anywhere in the product. Outcome positions can go to zero. You can lose the entire amount committed to a position. Probabilities shown are the market's price, not a forecast produced or endorsed by Polymarct, and a market being confident is not evidence that it is correct.

ELIGIBILITY

Prediction markets can engage gambling, derivatives, securities and financial promotion regimes depending on jurisdiction and market type. Access is subject to jurisdiction gating, age restriction, and identity checks where required. Some categories are restricted or unavailable in some jurisdictions.
