BRAND SYSTEM

The mark is
an argument.

One square is one unit of probability, the thing that pays out at settlement. The boundary running through it is not decoration, it is the price: the stepped path that separates the YES mass from the NO void. When the market reprices, the logo reprices.

01 / THE MARK

The split unit.

Pure geometry, no type inside the mark, no effects. Acid is reserved for the price line, never the mass: it is the only saturated colour in the identity and it marks the one thing the product sells.
